# Arcana The Assembly

This is a project born out of a concern for the survival of a game losing its way with in by giving into the alure of the beyond in the year of 2026. A contigency plan with hopes of lasting beyond the life span of comercial goods and to give a lasting place for the game into culture at large. With all that comes with a game becoming a cultural concept, including the decentralized nature of iteration, concensus, and even disagreement. Hence hosting such an open mana project on GitHub. Where it can be forked, merged, and edited to the people's desires.

## A New Model of Mana: The Spheres of Mana

For reasons, some slight touches and changes are necessary for the open mana project. Including a touch to the cosmic model of mana. This model is free to use beyond this project as well, because ideas like these shouldn't be locked to one project.

Mana flows through everything. It is the basis of all of existence in every plane, whether or not it is known to that plane. And while there are exceptions and planes of existence in which mana flows differently, most planes can be seen to observe a series of spheres of mana. It starts at a core and radiates outward.

Fire - Expression & Chaos (Red): At the very core, the start of all things, there is fire. Powerful and untamed, it is as likely to create the world anew as it is to destroy it.

Water - Flexibility & Intellectualism (Blue): Floating above the core, the tempering of all things, there is water. Clarity and fluidity, it guides the world to new ways as being.

Earth - Life & Tradition (Green): Rising above the sea, the foundation of all things, there is earth. Fertile and unmoving, it shelters the world in its comforting grasp.

Air - Order & Purity (Orange): Gusting above the land, the aspiration of all things, there is air. Pure and motivated, it moves the world in its sole directive.

Void - Spirituality & Death: Idling beyond the air, the finality of all things, there is void. Immaterial and lifeless, it guides the soul of the world back to the beginning.

Mana loops in and upon itself, from Void back into Fire, making a complete cycle of mana. Indeed this cycle gives rise to natural alliances of rivalries between the manas, with each one being friendly to its neighbors in the cycle and hostile to the others.
